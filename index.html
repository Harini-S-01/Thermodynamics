The Science Behind This App

  • Usage: Cover the front camera fully with your finger. Don't move or press hard. Allow time for pulse detection.
  • Photoplethysmography (PPG): Detects changes in blood volume via light absorption using the camera.
  • Thermodynamics Visualization: BPM controls the pattern states — solid (low), liquid (normal), gas (high) — showing energy and entropy.
  • Entropy: Represents disorder increasing with pulse rate, reflected as more complex visuals.
  • Biophysical Education: Connects heart biology and physics interactively through art.
